GIG Logistics Products

Discover reliable logistics solutions for your personal, business, and corporate needs.

Domestic Shipping

GIG Logistics offers fast, affordable, and personalised domestic delivery
services across Nigeria, with over 170 experience centres ensuring
nationwide coverage. Ship via the GIGGo App or visit any GIGL Experience
Centre nationwide.

Last-Mile Delivery

Need your package delivered to your doorstep? We will ship it! At GIG Logistics, we handle the final stretch so you don’t have to stress. Our last‑mile delivery service covers over 18 states across Nigeria, and goes even further, reaching doorsteps in the UK, USA, Canada, and Ghana.

Export to 230+ Locations Worldwide

Expanding to global markets or sending items to loved ones? With GIG Logistics export shipping, you can ship from Nigeria & Ghana to over 230 locations worldwide, including the UK, USA, Canada, and China. Visit any GIGL Experience Centre in Nigeria/Ghana or download the GIGGo App.

Import to Nigeria & Ghana

Shop and ship from stores in the UK, USA, China, or Canada to Nigeria & Ghana with ease. With a customer-first mindset and trusted global partners, we simplify cross-border shipping for you. Visit any GIGL Experience Centre in the UK, USA, China, or Canada, or download the GIGGo App for convenience.
