ADVICE ON SOLVING YOUTH UNEMPLOYMENT THROUGH SCHOOL ENGAGEMENT AND ATTRACTIVE FARMING INITIATIVES

Dear Sir,

I am writing to bring to your attention the growing rate of unemployment among Nigerian youths and to respectfully offer some suggestions on how your ministry can help address this challenge through agriculture.

Many young school leavers complete their education without stable job prospects, largely because farming-despite its huge potential-is still seen by many youths as unattractive, tiring, and unrewarding. I believe this perception can be changed if handled poperly, and your ministry is well placed to lead that change.

Firstly, I suggest that the Ministry partners directly with secondary schools and school leaders across the country to introduce agricultural entepreneurship talks and career sessions. Many studnts are simply unaware of the real opportuniyies that exist in modern agriculture, such as agribusiness, agro-processing and export farming. Inviting successful young farmers to speak to students, or organizing school farm visits, would help students see farming as a genuine career path rather than a last escort.

Secondly, to make farming attractive to youths, I recommend that the ministry introduces accessible loans, grants, and free access to farmland for young people who show interest in agriculture. Youths are more likely to embrace farming when they see it as modern and profitable, so promoting mechanized farming methods, irrigation technology and mobile apps for market access would help remove the old image of farming as purely manual, low-income labour.

Lastly, I suggest that the ministry runs an awareness campaign, possibly through social media, showcasing young Nigerians who have succeeded in agribusiness. This would inspure more youths to consider farming as a viable and dignified career.

I srtongly believe that if these steps are taken, more young people will see agriculture as a real solution to unemployment rather than something to avoid. Thank you for your time and attention to this matter.
